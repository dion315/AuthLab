Why sessions are revocable

Sessions are held server-side rather than in a self-contained token, so they can be ended immediately. That is what makes SCIM deprovisioning demonstrable: when a provider deactivates a user, their sessions are revoked on the spot instead of remaining valid until a token happens to expire.
